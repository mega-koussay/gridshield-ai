# References & Bibliography

All citations are real and publicly traceable. In the report they are cited as [n].

## False-data injection & smart-grid cyber-physical security

1. Liu, Y., Ning, P., & Reiter, M. K. (2011). *False data injection attacks against state estimation in electric power grids*. ACM Transactions on Information and System Security (TISSEC), 14(2), 1–33. doi:10.1145/1970321.1970328
2. Mo, Y., Sinopoli, B., et al. (2010/2014). *Cyber–physical security of a smart grid infrastructure*. Proceedings of the IEEE, 100(1), 195–209. doi:10.1109/JPROC.2011.5754690
3. Hink, R. C. B., et al. (2014). *Machine learning for power system disturbance and cyber-attack discrimination*. 7th International Symposium on Resilient Control Systems (ISRCS). doi:10.1109/ISRCS.2014.6909257
4. ESMARTGRID / Iowa State University. *Power system attack datasets* (smart-meter false-data-injection and attack scenarios). https://docs.datadrivendiscovery.org — widely used labeled smart-grid attack corpus.

## Anomaly detection & intrusion detection in energy systems

## Resilience & self-healing

14. Panteli, M., & Mancarella, P. (2017). *Modeling and evaluating the resilience of critical electrical power infrastructure to extreme weather events*. IEEE Systems Journal, 11(3), 1733–1742. doi:10.1109/JSYST.2015.2389272
15. NIST (2014). *Framework for Improving Critical Infrastructure Cybersecurity* (CSF — Identify/Protect/Detect/Respond/Recover). https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework

## Standards & datasets used for realism checks

16. NREL. *National Solar Radiation Data Base (NSRDB)* — reference for realistic clear-sky irradiance shape and intermittency magnitudes. https://nsrdb.nrel.gov
17. IEC 61850 / IEC 62351 — SCADA/ICS communication and security standards (context for the SCADA attack channel).

---

**Honesty notes**
- The microgrid dataset is **synthetic**, generated by this repo's seeded simulator
  (`scripts/generate_dataset.py`); it is not claimed to be a public dataset.
- NREL NSRDB (16) is referenced as the source for the *shape and scale* of solar
  intermittency used in the parametric model; no external download is required.
- Iowa State / ESMARTGRID attack data (4) influenced the attack taxonomy; the
  implemented attacks are re-implemented parametrically in `app/twin/attacks.py`.
